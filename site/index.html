Privacy

It reacts to how loud the app is, not to what it says.

No microphone is needed by default. No recording, transcript or server either. If you opt into “Pause while I speak”, Oreja uses your microphone only to keep playback interrupted.

Read the privacy note

  • Audio levels exist for milliseconds in memory and are never written to disk.
  • No account, no API key, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting.
  • Diagnostics keep Oreja's own state changes in memory, and forget them when it quits.
  • Oreja never launches Music, resets a track or clears a queue. Volume changes happen only in lower-volume mode.

The short answers.

Does Oreja record or transcribe what ChatGPT says?

No. It reads the output level of the selected application's audio and keeps only a boolean “active” state. No samples are stored, no speech is recognised, and no content leaves the Mac. There is no networking code in the app.

Do I need an OpenAI account or API key?

No. Oreja does not talk to OpenAI at all. It observes the local audio output of the app you choose, and controls Apple Music through macOS automation.

Why only Apple Music?

Apple Music is the only provider in this release. Other players would need their own adapter, capability declarations and tests; guessing at provider state is not something Oreja does. The music adapter is a port, so another provider is possible later.

Can I pause Music or lower its volume?

Yes. Choose pause mode to stop Music while the AI speaks and resume it afterward, or choose lower-volume mode to keep Music playing quietly and restore its original volume when the reply ends. The setting is yours.

Will a notification sound pause my music?

Possibly. Oreja reacts to output activity, not verified speech, so a sound played by the same application counts. Browser sources are worse: they capture every tab in that browser. Use the desktop app, or a dedicated browser window.

What if I pause or resume music myself?

Your choice wins. If you pause Music, Oreja will not resume it. If you resume Music while ChatGPT is speaking, Oreja releases its claim and waits for the next activity before acting again.

What happens if Oreja quits or crashes?

Quitting while Oreja owns a pause resumes the music first. After a crash, the pause journal restores playback on the next launch only when the same track is still paused and the pause is recent. If the track changed or Music quit, Oreja deliberately leaves it alone.

Is it notarized? Is it affiliated with OpenAI or Apple?

It is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so another Mac may need a one-time Gatekeeper approval. It is an independent project: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by OpenAI or Apple.
