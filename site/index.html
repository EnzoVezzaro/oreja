How it works

Three quiet steps. None of them yours.

Oreja watches the audio output of one application, decides once, and hands playback back. The decision lives in a single tested state machine, not in a view.

01

Listen

A Core Audio process tap copies the selected app's output. Only a level is computed, in memory, and discarded immediately.

02

Decide

One deterministic coordinator weighs activity, silence and your own changes. Choose whether it pauses Music or lowers its volume through the gaps between sentences.

03

Hand back

A single Apple Event pauses or resumes Music, or sets and restores its volume. Track, queue and position stay intact, and manual control always wins.

Privacy

It measures a level, not a conversation.

No microphone is needed by default. No recording, transcript or server either. If you opt into “Pause while I speak,” Oreja uses your microphone only to keep playback interrupted.

Read the privacy note →
  • Audio levels exist for milliseconds in memory and are never written to disk.
  • No account, no API key, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting.
  • Diagnostics keep Oreja's own state changes in memory, and forget them when it quits.
  • Oreja never launches Music, resets a track or clears a queue. Volume changes happen only when you choose lower-volume mode.

Verification

The honest version.

A small app that claims little is easier to trust. This is what has actually been executed on the development machine, and what has not.

Executed

  • The automated suite covers the coordinator, detector, settings, pause journal and AppleScript parsing.
  • The app bundle builds, signs, launches, and stays resident in the menu bar.
  • Core Audio process tap capture was verified with a local probe against a real audio-producing process.
  • Apple Music pause and resume were verified end to end with the integration harness and a controlled audio fixture.

Not yet

  • A live ChatGPT Voice session has not been run. The ChatGPT audio path is unverified against real replies.
  • CPU and battery impact, Bluetooth and AirPods switching, and live end-to-end latency are unmeasured.
  • The build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
  • Browser sources are experimental and capture every tab in that browser.

Commands, raw output and dates live in docs/test-evidence.md in the repository.

Get Oreja

Build it in one command.

There is no notarized download yet. Oreja is a local, ad-hoc signed build: from a checkout of the repository, run the script, then open the app. 

./scripts/build.sh
open build/Oreja.app

On another Mac, Gatekeeper may warn because the build carries no Developer ID. Build locally, or allow it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Oreja does not disable Gatekeeper.

  • ·macOS 14.2 or newer. Core Audio process taps require it.
  • ·ChatGPT desktop app, or ChatGPT in Safari/Chrome as an experimental source.
  • ·The Music app, already playing something. Oreja never opens it for you.
  • ·Command Line Tools if you build from source. No third-party dependencies.

Questions

The short answers.

Does Oreja record or transcribe what ChatGPT says?

No. It reads the output level of the selected application's audio and keeps only a boolean "active" state. No samples are stored, no speech is recognised, and no content leaves the Mac. There is no networking code in the app.

Do I need an OpenAI account or API key?

No. Oreja does not talk to OpenAI at all. It observes the local audio output of the app you choose, and controls Apple Music through macOS automation.

Why only Apple Music?

Apple Music is the only provider in this release. Other players would need their own adapter, capability declarations and tests; guessing at provider state is not something Oreja does. The music adapter is a port, so another provider is possible later.

Can I pause Music or lower its volume?

Yes. Choose pause mode to stop Music while the AI speaks and resume it afterward, or choose lower-volume mode to keep Music playing quietly and restore its original volume when the reply ends. The setting is yours.

Will a notification sound pause my music?

Possibly. Oreja reacts to output activity, not verified speech, so a sound played by the same application counts. Browser sources are worse: they capture every tab in that browser. Use the desktop app, or a dedicated browser window.

What if I pause or resume music myself?

Your choice wins. If you pause Music, Oreja will not resume it. If you resume Music while ChatGPT is speaking, Oreja releases its claim and waits for the next activity before acting again.

What happens if Oreja quits or crashes?

Quitting while Oreja owns a pause resumes the music first. After a crash, the pause journal restores playback on the next launch only when the same track is still paused and the pause is recent. If the track changed or Music quit, Oreja deliberately leaves it alone.

Is it notarized? Is it affiliated with OpenAI or Apple?

It is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so another Mac may need a one-time Gatekeeper approval. It is an independent project: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by OpenAI or Apple.
