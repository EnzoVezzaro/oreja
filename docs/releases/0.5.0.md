# Oreja 0.5.0

**Released:** 2026-11-22  
**Minimum macOS:** 14.2  
**Distribution:** ad-hoc signed, not notarized

Oreja 0.5.0 is the stable release of the audio repair. Your AI talks, or you talk, and your audio
makes room: Apple Music pauses or lowers, and any other app (a browser playing YouTube, Spotify,
VLC) is lowered or silenced.

## What works

| Conversation | Music | Result |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT Desktop | Apple Music | Pauses or lowers, then restores (maker-confirmed live) |
| Your voice | Apple Music | Pauses or lowers, then restores (maker-confirmed live) |
| Your voice / ChatGPT Desktop | YouTube in a browser | Lowered to the duck level, then restored (observed live in the app log and measured; ChatGPT-triggered run maker-confirmed) |
| ChatGPT Web in one browser | Music in another browser or app | Same mechanism as above (not measured separately) |
| ChatGPT Web | Music in the same browser | Not possible: one browser is one audio process |

## What's new

- **Lower these apps** (Playback): browsers (except Safari), Spotify, VLC and any other player are
  lowered to your level while someone speaks, or silenced in pause mode, then ramped back. Core
  Audio process taps only: no browser setting, no page scripting, no system volume.
- **Your voice**: recalibrated thresholds; listens on the headset mic while a voice session or call
  holds it, otherwise on the Mac's own mic, so Oreja never puts a Bluetooth headset into call mode
  by itself. On the Mac's speakers your voice only extends an interruption already in progress.
- **Status names the speaker**: "ChatGPT is speaking", "You are speaking", "You and ChatGPT are
  speaking".
- **Test interruption** button, **permission status** (audio recording, microphone, Music
  automation), **diagnostics shortcuts**, and a warning when a source is playing media.
- **Redesigned Settings**: Audio = what Oreja listens to; Playback = what makes room. Compact app
  lists with search, aligned switches, live Apple Music status.
- **The version in the app is stamped from git**, so it always matches the running build.

## Fixed

- A silent tap or an audio-route change (headphones, AirPods, sample rate) no longer leaves
  Oreja listening to a dead tap: the tap is rebuilt only when frames actually stop, with a settle
  window and cooldown, so a healthy tap survives a device switch.
- A live tap with no audio for 30 seconds records an actionable Screen & System Audio Recording
  hint instead of guessing at the permission. It stays diagnostic only.

## Tests

- Automated: `./scripts/test.sh` — 122 passed, 0 failed, 571 assertions (including nine
  monitor-recovery tests).
- Build: `./scripts/build.sh` — ad-hoc signature verified; app launched with only its own
  frameworks loaded (~50–56 MB footprint).
- Live, on macOS 26.6.2 (Apple M2), Sony Bluetooth headphones:
  - The maker reported Apple Music lowering while ChatGPT Desktop spoke and while they spoke.
  - YouTube in ego lite: a real voice onset produced "Lowering ego lite to 11% during speech.", the
    replayed audio measured about −40 dBFS (original about −15 dBFS), then "Restored ego lite." and
    Oreja's output stopped.
  - Microphone: headset speech measured −21 to −25 dBFS (threshold −35); the monitor moved to the
    headset mic while a ChatGPT voice session held it.
  - Real-time renderer reviewed independently (no allocation or reference counting in the audio
    callback; time-based 0.2 s ramp).

Not measured: ChatGPT Web + Apple Music, Spotify and VLC (not installed on the test Mac), CPU and
battery impact. Full evidence: [test-evidence.md](../test-evidence.md).

## Limitations

- Other apps are lowered or silenced, not paused; in pause mode a video keeps playing silently.
- Safari cannot be lowered: its audio runs in a WebKit process shared with other apps.
- ChatGPT Web and music in the same browser cannot be separated. Use two browsers.
- Ad-hoc signed and not notarized: the first launch on another Mac needs one Gatekeeper approval
  (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway).
